Privacy Policy

Prayer Pilot · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Prayer Pilot does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking in the app.

Location

The app asks for your location while you are using it, only to calculate prayer times for where you are. Your coordinates are used on your device. They are not sent to us. If you search for a city manually, the place name is converted to coordinates by the location services of your phone's operating system (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (calculation method, madhab, time adjustments, chosen location and notification choices) are saved locally on your device only. Deleting the app removes this data.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not use any server, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
