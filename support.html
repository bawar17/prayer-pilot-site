Prayer Pilot

Need help or want to report a problem? Email bawar17@gmail.com and include your phone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Common questions

The times differ from my local mosque. Open Settings, then Time Adjustments, and add or subtract minutes for each prayer. You can also change the calculation method and madhab.

I don't get prayer reminders. Check that notifications are allowed for Prayer Pilot in your phone's settings, and that reminders are on in the app.

Wrong location. Make sure location access is allowed while using the app, or set your location manually in Settings.
